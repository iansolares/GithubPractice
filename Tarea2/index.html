  1. Shutter Island

    2. Datos Generales

    Shutter Island is a 2010 American neo-noir psychological thriller film directed by Martin Scorsese. The screenplay was adapted by Laeta Kalogridis from the 2003 novel by Dennis Lehane. It follows Deputy U.S. Marshal Edward "Teddy" Daniels and his partner Chuck, who come to the fictional Shutter Island in Boston Harbor to investigate its criminal psychiatric facility after one of its patients goes missing; Daniels has his own ulterior motives for taking the case.

    Actores destacados

    Algunos de los actores que están en esta película son:

    • Leonardo DiCaprio
    • Mark Ruffalo
    • Ben Kingsley
    Dato Curioso

    Shutter Island was released with The Ghost Writer, and, earning $41 million in its first weekend, finished first at the box office and gave Scorsese his highest-grossing box office opening to date.

    Premios

    Shutter Island ganó 11 premios y recibió 66 nominaciones. Ganó:

    • 2010 NBR Award Best Production Design
    • 2010 NBR Award Top Ten Films
    • 2010 SDFCS Award Best Production Design
    • 2010 Teen Choice Award Choice Movie Actor: Horror/Thriller (Leonardo DiCaprio)
    • 2010 Scream Award Best Scream-Play (Laeta Kalogridis)
    • 2010 IOMA Best Actor (Leonardo DiCaprio)
    • 2011 Georges Award Best Foreign Drama Movie
    • 2010 Film Award Best Picture
    • 2010 Best Supporting Voice Actor (Dario Penne)
